# Launcher wallpapers

The eighteen entries in `SOURCES.json` each have a background JPEG, a thumbnail and a small blurred panel backdrop. Four registered material JPEGs per image live in `../materials/`. All derived images retain the source photograph's terms. Three entries (Rolling surf, Dusk coast, Rain window) also carry a `video` loop: a muted 15-second MP4 that plays over the still photo, whose poster frame is the background JPEG; their video sources and licences are recorded in `SOURCES.json` and [asset sources](../../../docs/asset-sources.md).

Photo pages, authors and licence observations are in [asset sources](../../../docs/asset-sources.md). Generation instructions are in [asset generation](../../../docs/assets.md). Full-size originals remain outside the repository.
