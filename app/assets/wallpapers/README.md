# Launcher wallpapers

The eighteen entries in `SOURCES.json` each have a background JPEG, a thumbnail and a small blurred panel backdrop. Four registered material JPEGs per image live in `../materials/`. All derived images retain the source photograph's terms. Three entries (Rolling surf, Dusk coast, Rain window) also carry a `video` loop: a muted HD H.264 MP4 that plays over the still photo, whose poster frame is the background JPEG, plus a `video4k` 3840×2160 HEVC MP4 of the same scene that Settings → Background → Live video quality selects (4K by default, HD fallback). Their video sources and licences are recorded in `SOURCES.json` and [asset sources](../../../docs/asset-sources.md).

Photo pages, authors and licence observations are in [asset sources](../../../docs/asset-sources.md). Generation instructions are in [asset generation](../../../docs/assets.md). Full-size originals remain outside the repository.
